Canadian hazard classification

Independent Canadian Hazardous Products Regulations (SOR/2015-17) calculations. This pilot covers oral/dermal acute toxicity ATE, additive skin/eye ingredient screening and HHNOC ingredient triggers. Tested-mixture/bridging evidence and non-additive special-ingredient routes require separate review. It is not a complete WHMIS classification.

Check whole-mixture evidence and bridging principles before accepting ingredient-based results. Absence of a result for an unimplemented hazard does not mean the product is unclassified.

Ingredient formulation

ATE values are mg/kg. For an acute toxicity category without ATE, enter 1–4. Explicitly select “No acute hazard evidence” only with substantiation. Skin/eye categories use HPR 8.2.11 and 8.3.11 additive rules; tick “Special?” for non-additive chemistry requiring separate review.
