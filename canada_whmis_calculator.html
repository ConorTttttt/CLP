Read calculation scope and regulatory limitations
Ingredient-based oral and dermal acute toxicity, skin/eye additive mixtures and HHNOC ingredient triggers are implemented. Whole-mixture data, bridging, inhalation, special non-additive chemistry, other health and physical hazard classes, Schedule 4 prescribed classifications and bilingual WHMIS labelling are not yet automated. A missing result does not establish that a product is non-hazardous.

1. Mixture information

Mass % w/w Fixed in pilot

Ingredient concentrations must total 100%. Gas inhalation uses a separate % v/v field for each ingredient; other implemented health pathways use % w/w.

2. Canadian HPA supplier scope

Preliminary federal supplier applicability screen. Exclusions must be supported by evidence; an excluded product may still be hazardous or subject to other rules.

3. Acute toxicity — inhalation (HPR 8.1)

Select gas, vapour or dust/mist. The substance LC₅₀ is entered per ingredient under Edit details. Tested-mixture data take precedence over bridging and ingredient additivity.

Other inhalation exceptions, including toxic gases released on contact with water, still require expert review.

4. Chemicals Under Pressure (HPR 7.21)

Requires defensible package and composition evidence. A GHS Rev.8 category is only proposed if the HPR eligibility conditions and data are complete.

5. Ingredients

Enter ingredient concentrations and their individual oral, dermal, skin, eye and HHNOC information. Each ingredient has a compact hazard overview. Select Edit details for grouped fields. Blank acute toxicity fields are unknown, not evidence of safety.

Regulatory references and limitations

Based on the Hazardous Products Regulations, SOR/2015-17, especially sections 2.2, 8.1.1–8.1.7, 8.2.9–8.2.11, 8.3.9–8.3.11 and 8.12.4.

This is a limited pilot. Only inhalation has a limited tested-mixture/bridging input. Oral/dermal, skin/eye and special ingredient pathways still require additional evidence and review.

Canadian WHMIS 2022 classification pilot. Regulatory reference: English HPR XML consolidated 1 January 2023. English/French compliant WHMIS labels and SDSs are not generated.
