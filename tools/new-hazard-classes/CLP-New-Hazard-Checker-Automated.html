Automatic numerical criteria screening

Enter trustworthy experimental measurements. The software checks the precise CLP inequality limits for P/vP, B/vB, M/vM and one aquatic T route. Positive results are provisional evidence indications, not expert-approved conclusions.

For ionisable substances, the lowest log Koc across pH 4–9 is required. PBT/PMT toxicity can also be established through other CLP endpoints not represented by the numerical toxicity field. A “no numerical trigger” result never establishes non-classification.

Guided regulatory decisions

Finish only the hazard groups for which sufficient evidence is available. Every decision needs an Annex VI check, regulatory basis, source, scientific rationale and explicit reviewer sign-off.

Decision order: (1) Verify the applicable Annex VI entry and substance identity. (2) Examine and weigh the relevant studies and other reliable evidence. (3) Determine each hazard-class outcome, including a justified “not classified” where supported. (4) Sign off and save to the local library. If information is insufficient, leave “Unresolved”.

Substance classification requires more than three yes/no answers

For endocrine disruption, evidence strength, adverse-effect relevance and the biologically plausible link determine the final category. For PBT, vPvB, PMT and vPvM, apply all legal criteria, additional evidence and weight-of-evidence provisions. Always check harmonised Annex VI classifications and assess the remaining hazards.

Documented limitations of this version

No live ECHA data retrieval or automatic verification of regulatory claims; a locally reviewed substance library is supported. No automatic scientific weight-of-evidence determination, gas-mixture calculation, whole-mixture endocrine evidence adjudication, bridging-rule adjudication or final label optimisation. “No ingredient trigger” does not equal a formal conclusion of “not classified”.
