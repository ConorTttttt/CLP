CLP Mixture Classification Calculator

Regulation (EC) 1272/2008 Annex I, consolidated 01.07.2026 · ingredient-based method · works offline, no AI needed
Decision-support only. You supply the ingredient classifications (from Annex VI / C&L Inventory / SDS); the calculator applies the Annex I mixture rules and shows every step. It does not model bridging principles, tested-mixture data, physical hazards other than flammable liquids, independent validation of manual hazard classifications under Article 26(2), full Article 27 case-by-case redundancy review, or Article 28 P-statement selection. It applies Article 26 pictogram precedence and explicit Article 27 Annex III H/EUH omission pairs, each with an audit trail. Verify against the official text before relying on a result.

Mixture

Flash point, viscosity and pH belong to the mixture (measured or calculated). Leave blank if unknown — the matching hazard is then reported as "no data".

Ingredients

Type the ingredient's H-codes (e.g. H302 H315 H318 H410) and the classes are filled in. Use Details to set categories, ATE values, M-factors, specific concentration limits (SCLs) and exceptions the codes cannot express (e.g. Skin Corr. 1B, Acute Tox. 1 vs 2).
