Read calculation scope and regulatory limitations
You supply the ingredient classifications (from Annex VI / C&L Inventory / SDS); the calculator applies its implemented Annex I mixture rules and shows every step. It does not model bridging principles, tested-mixture data, physical hazards other than flammable liquids, independent validation of manual hazard classifications under Article 26(2), full Article 27 case-by-case redundancy review, or complete Article 28 P-statement selection. It applies Article 26 pictogram precedence, explicit Article 27 Annex III H/EUH omission pairs, and a conservative Article 28 candidate selector, each with an audit trail. Verify against the official text before relying on a result.

3. Labelling context Article 28: audience, use, disposal and P-statements
Precautionary statements depend on intended use, the supply audience, the hazard classification and Annex IV criteria. This is a partial, auditable candidate selector — not a final approved label.
Consumer disposal: Article 28(2) normally requires a disposal statement covering contents and packaging, subject to Article 22. This is a category-level requirement, not a claim that every candidate P-code is mandatory. Annex IV wording with unresolved choices must be completed before release.
4. Manual classifications From measured data or independently determined hazards
Anything you determined yourself — e.g. Oxid. Liq. 3, Eye Irrit. 2 from a test. It is added to the label summary as entered, not calculated.
