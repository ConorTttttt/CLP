Read calculation scope and regulatory limitations

The ABNT NBR 14725:2023 text was not available when this module was written. Thresholds come from the UN GHS Rev. 7 mixture criteria that the standard is based on; building-block adoption and the generic cut-off option must be verified against the NBR text. Physical hazards other than flammable liquids, whole-mixture test data, bridging principles, specific concentration limits, Portuguese label wording, precautionary statements and the FDS are not generated. A missing result does not establish that a product is non-hazardous.

Regulatory scope and sources

  • ABNT NBR 14725:2023 — Produtos químicos: informações sobre segurança, saúde e meio ambiente. Consolidates former parts 1–4 (terminology, classification, labelling, FDS); published 2023-07-03, mandatory from 2025-07-04. Based on UN GHS Rev. 7.
  • NR-26 (Ministério do Trabalho e Emprego) — requires hazardous chemicals at work to be classified, labelled and accompanied by an FDS in accordance with NBR 14725.
  • UN GHS Rev. 7 (ST/SG/AC.10/30/Rev.7) — source of the mixture criteria implemented here.
  • Transport (ANTT), pesticides, ANVISA-regulated products, medicines and cosmetics may follow separate rules.
